# Lordaeron concept set: notes (village and town tiers)

Eight views of Hearthglen, painted from the approved street sketch: for each tier, the town from the road, a street,
the forum and the abbey. For `assets-src/concept/lordaeron/<tier>/`.

## Materials

- **Walls:** dark oak close-studding over white plaster (calcite, white concrete) on a cobblestone plinth. Brick
  nogging on the better town houses.
- **Upper storeys:** jettied one block on bracketed dark oak beams (town tier; the village is mostly single storey).
- **Roofs:** thatch (hay, with a brown terracotta ridge) for cottages; red clay tile (brick and granite stairs) for
  town houses and halls. A street mixes both: the tile marks the richer house.
- **Chimneys:** brick, set into the side wall from the hearth, solid through the roof.
- **Stone:** grey stone brick and cobblestone for the chapel, the abbey, the town wall and the well-house base.
- **Heraldry:** Lordaeron blue and gold banners on the town gate and the forum.

## Palettes

- **daub:** dark oak, white plaster, thatch, cobblestone. Village default.
- **brick:** dark oak, brick infill, red tile. The town's better houses and halls.
- **stone:** stone brick ground storey, timber above, tile. Corner houses and anything on the forum.

## By tier

| | Village | Town |
|---|---|---|
| Homes | storey-and-a-half cottages, thatch, kitchen gardens | two storeys, jettied, tile or thatch, trade yards |
| Wall | log palisade, timber gatehouse with tile roof | stone curtain, square towers, gatehouse with banners |
| Forum | bell in a timber well-house, a stone well, three or four striped stalls | the bell in a tall timber belfry on a stone plinth, a ring of stalls, lamps, planted trees |
| Abbey | a Norman church with a square tower and a few ranges | Perpendicular Gothic church with a pinnacled tower, cloister, gatehouse |
| Approach | a timber bridge, a path through strip fields | the bridge with lamp posts, the road to the gate |

## Signs of trade

- **Shepherd:** dye vats made of barrels filled with coloured water (not cauldrons), fleeces drying on a rack.
- **Smith:** anvil, quenching trough, iron stock.
- **Farmer:** domed brick bake oven with loaves on a board.
- **Stalls:** goods on show: bread, wool, tools, fish.

## Planting

Oaks in the gaps between houses and along the town's avenues; orchards on the abbey hill; hedges round kitchen gardens;
flower boxes under every window; strip fields with wheat outside the palisade.

## Where the builds will be tighter than the paintings

- **Lanes:** they read 4 to 6 wide. Yours are 2, with a doorstep each side; the builds follow the plan.
- **Spacing:** houses one block apart, not shoulder to shoulder.
- **The town-tier abbey** is painted cathedral-sized. It will be a landmark in parts (your 48-block template limit),
  with this composition and this silhouette at a smaller scale.
- **The town wall** is painted taller than a town-tier curtain. The kit will use the gatehouse and tower language at
  your heights.
