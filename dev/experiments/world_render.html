On the map

Towns founded by this day

The film

An MP4 for posting: Discord plays it inline.

A pixel is a block. Drag to pan, scroll or pinch to zoom. The roads are the paths the caravans wore into the land. Each caravan is on the map from the day it left to the day it was home, out to the far town and back (squares are wagons, rounds are llama trains). The clock starts the day before the first caravan left and runs 1.5 seconds a day while any is on the road, faster through quiet days. Where the run kept its world on several days, the land is shown as it stood on the latest of them, so the towns grow as the clock runs; each town's name shows from the day it was founded.
